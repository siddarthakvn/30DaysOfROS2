# Day 28 — Interview questions

1. A detection gives column 276.522 and row 250.870. What else do you need before that can be a Nav2 goal?

2. `projectPixelTo3dRay` returns a unit ray. Why is multiplying it by the optical-axis depth the wrong scale, and what scale does this day use?

3. The camera point is `(-0.200, 0.050, 2.300)` and the message says `frame_id: map`. Where does the goal end up, and why?

4. The optical frame's origin echoes at `(1.200, 2.000, 0.300)` in the map. How is that number built from the base pose and the camera mount?

5. Why is the optical frame rotated `-90, 0, -90` degrees relative to a forward-looking camera link?

6. This run reports `error_m=0.000000`. What does that prove, and what does it leave unmeasured?
