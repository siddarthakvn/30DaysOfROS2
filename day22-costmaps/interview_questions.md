# Day 22 — Interview questions

1. The simulator shows empty floor, and the planned path bends around a spot in the middle. Which map did the planner read?

2. A cell was marked at 2.3 m and the robot is now 3.2 m away. `raytrace_max_range` is 3.0 m. Why is the cell still lethal?

3. What is the difference between cost 254, cost 253, and a cost between 1 and 252?

4. Inflation is off, and the path passes 5 cm from a lethal cell. The robot radius is 0.22 m. What happens to the body?

5. The local costmap is a 3 m rolling window and does not contain the phantom. Why can the global path still avoid it?

6. You want the straight path back. Which cell has to change, and which layer is allowed to clear it?
