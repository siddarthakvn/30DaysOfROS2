# Day 24 — Interview questions

1. A box appears on the global path. Why can `/plan` still be the old line?

2. The local controller goes around the box and the global path does not change. Which stage actually avoided the obstacle?

3. The same controller stops in front of a wider box. What limit did the box exceed?

4. After a replan, which message changes, and which stage consumes it?

5. Nav2's navigate-to-pose tree can call `ComputePathToPose` more than once. What failure of `FollowPath` makes that second call useful?

6. The body is 0.22 m in radius and passes 0.019 m clear of the wide box. What would a negative clearance mean?
