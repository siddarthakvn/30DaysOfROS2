```text
I asked reality to wait. It didn’t.

That’s what I was secretly hoping for.

My “camera” was firing at 30 Hz.
My “brain” could only think at ~10 Hz.
So I did the classic fix: raise the queue. Don’t drop frames. Catch up later.

Reality did not slow down.
I did.

30 in. 10 out.
The missing frames didn’t vanish — they made me late.

Same overload. One knob:

depth 1  → ~3 behind reality
depth 10 → ~12 behind reality

ROS 2 won’t pause the world for your slow callback.
It buffers. Drops the oldest. Lets you feel busy while you process the past.

Bigger queue ≠ safer.
Bigger queue = permission to be late.

This is where we answer today’s question:
What happens when reality produces information faster than your robot can understand it?
```
